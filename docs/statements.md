## A defect found: the cash field lags a year

EODHD gives cash twice, as `cash` and `cashAndEquivalents`. On 242 yearly balance sheets since 2015, in 108 payloads, only `cashAndEquivalents` plus short-term investments equals EODHD's own total of cash and short-term investments; `cash` does not. On AMD's 2021 balance sheet, `cash` is 1,595 million dollars, the 2020 balance, and `cashAndEquivalents` is 2,535 million, the real one. The load stores `cash` as decided in ADR 0029, so these 242 rows hold a wrong cash figure. Nothing reports it yet.

## Against annual reports

20 stored fiscal years were compared with the companies' own annual reports: 10-K filings on sec.gov, and for the rest the reports filed with cninfo, the ASX, DART and the TWSE, or published on the company's site. The years were picked by a fixed rule, not by hand: in each region, the first by a hash of exchange code, symbol and date. Half of them were years whose interim rows do not add up to the year, the other half years whose interim rows do. Four figures were compared for each: revenue, net income attributable to the company's shareholders, operating cash flow and total assets. The agents that read the reports were not told the stored figures.

| Result | Fiscal years |
|---|---|
| Every figure agrees, within the provider's rounding of a few units | 14: AIB Data Centers, Maravai, N-able and Standex in the US; Yihai Kerry, Zhuhai Huafa, Chengxin Lithium and Zhejiang Jiemei in China; Vallourec, Unilever and AkzoNobel in Europe; Deep Yellow, LG Chem and Ambassador Hotel. Unilever's total assets are 0.01% off |
| One figure differs | 2. Chubb's revenue is 49,997 million dollars against a reported 49,735 (0.5%). Anika's 2023 revenue is 120.8 million against 166.7 million in its 2023 report; the stored figure looks like the later re-presentation for continuing operations, which was not checked |
| The figures belong to another period | 2. Amentum: the figures are the predecessor's fiscal year to 29 September 2023, dated 2023-12-31. EQ Resources: the year is dated 2023-12-31, but its loss, operating cash flow and total assets are those of the year to 30 June 2024, and its revenue is 1.3% off that year's |
| A figure is wrong | 2. Octave Specialty Group (formerly Ambac): net income is −240.9 million dollars, against −259.1 million for the group and −261.7 million attributable. Storebrand: revenue is 207 million kroner, while the insurer reports no revenue line and its operating income is 14,588 million; net income is 5,046 million against 5,023 for the group and 5,018 attributable |

So 4 of the 20 years have something wrong that no check of ADR 0030 catches. Three of the four are among the 11 years whose interim rows do not add up, and one among the 9 whose interim rows do. Three of the six years with a difference are insurers or a financial guarantor, a kind of business the QARP screen leaves out. The sample was weighted towards odd years on purpose, so the rate is not a rate for the whole universe.

Of the original reports, five were later restated or re-presented by the companies themselves (Yihai Kerry, Vallourec, Unilever, N-able, and probably Anika). The stored figures are the original ones in all but Anika.

## What the next steps have to deal with

- **Interim rows.** The next step reads quarters and half-years into the same tables, proven against their fiscal year.
- **The corrections file.** The report is the work list; how an approved correction comes back into the tables is not decided.
- **The cash field.** A decision on the lagging cash field (above).
- **Years that belong to another period, and wrong figures of financial companies.** Four of the 20 years checked against annual reports have one (above). Neither is caught by a check today.
